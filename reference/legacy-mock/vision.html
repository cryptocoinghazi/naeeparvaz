Our purpose

Vision, mission & objectives

The principles that define what Naee Parvaz seeks to become, how it intends to serve the public and the standards by which its journalism should be judged.

Vision

An informed, aware and constructive public sphere.

To become a trusted news platform that helps people understand their world, participate in society and engage with public life with clarity and confidence.

Mission

Credible journalism with context, fairness and public purpose.

To report relevant facts, explain complex developments, amplify meaningful stories and host responsible debate while maintaining editorial independence.

Core objective

Make trustworthy information useful and accessible.

Every story should help readers know something important, understand it better or make a more informed judgement as citizens and community members.

Strategic objectives

What the organization will work toward

These objectives can later be translated into newsroom policies, editorial workflows and measurable organizational standards.

01

Credibility

Establish rigorous verification, sourcing, corrections and editorial-review practices.

02

Public Awareness

Explain issues that affect citizens, particularly those receiving insufficient attention.

03

Representation

Bring diverse regions, communities and credible viewpoints into public conversation responsibly.

04

Constructive Journalism

Go beyond describing problems by examining responses, evidence, consequences and possible solutions.

05

Media Literacy

Help audiences distinguish verified reporting from misinformation, propaganda, opinion and speculation.

06

Digital Innovation

Use video, data, visual storytelling and modern technology to make serious journalism more accessible.

Editorial principles

Standards behind the mission

These are the practical values that should guide reporting, editorial judgement and public accountability.

Independence

Editorial judgement without improper pressure

Political, commercial or personal interests should not dictate how facts are reported or which legitimate stories receive attention.

Fairness

Relevant perspectives represented accurately

Disagreements should be reported with rigor, proportionality and respect rather than false balance or sensational framing.

Transparency

Readers should know what is known—and unknown

Sources, evidence, limitations and corrections should be communicated as clearly as circumstances allow.

Responsible journalism for an informed, aware and constructive society.
