Introduction

About Naee Parvaz

A news media platform founded on the belief that credible information, constitutional awareness and responsible public dialogue are essential to a healthy society.

Who we are

A newsroom designed to earn trust.

Naee Parvaz News is being developed as a digital-first media outlet committed to reporting that is accurate, relevant and grounded in public interest. We believe audiences deserve journalism that respects their intelligence and gives them enough context to understand why a story matters.

The identity of Naee Parvaz reflects the idea of a “new flight”: moving forward with fresh energy while remaining rooted in truth, constitutional values and social responsibility.

Our long-term ambition is to become a trusted source for citizens seeking serious reporting, local and national perspectives, accountable public-interest journalism and constructive debate.

Editorial promise

What readers should expect

Our credibility will depend on consistent standards, not slogans. These principles are intended to guide editorial decisions as the newsroom grows.

Reporting

Clear separation of fact and opinion

News reporting, analysis and commentary should be clearly identified so readers understand the nature of what they are reading.

Sourcing

Evidence and attribution

We should identify reliable sources, verify claims and communicate uncertainty honestly where facts remain incomplete.

Accountability

Corrections without hesitation

Errors should be corrected transparently and promptly because trust requires the newsroom to be accountable to its audience.
